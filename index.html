Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 28, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Saw Capital, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application, Noema - Feel the Calm (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

To provide a functional experience, we collect the following:

  • Account Data: Your email address and name provided during sign-up.
  • User Content: Quotes or data you choose to "save" or interact with within the app.
  • Usage Data: Basic technical information about how you interact with the app, provided by our hosting platform, Adalo.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Create and maintain your user account.
  • Save your preferences (such as saved quotes).
  • Send occasional push notifications (only if you have opted in).
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Adalo to host our application and database. Your data is stored securely on their servers. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Security

The security of your personal information is important to us. We use the security tools provided by our hosting platform, Adalo, to protect your data, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. GDPR (For European Users)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users in the EU have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request that we correct or delete your personal data.
  • Object to the processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at SmoothChillPill@gmail.com.

7. CCPA (For California Users)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected.
  • Request deletion of their personal information.
  • Opt-out of any "sale" of personal information (Note: We do not sell your data).

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information as long as your account is active.

Account Deletion: You may delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the App Settings or by contacting us at SmoothChillPill@gmail.com. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

9. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.
