Last Updated: January 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Saw Capital, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application, Noema - Feel the Calm.

To provide a functional experience, we collect the following:

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

4. Third-Party Services

We use Adalo to host our application and database. Your data is stored securely on their servers. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. GDPR (For European Users)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users in the EU have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request that we correct or delete your personal data.
  • Object to the processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at SmoothChillPill@gmail.com.

6. CCPA (For California Users)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected.
  • Request deletion of their personal information.
  • Opt-out of any "sale" of personal information (Note: We do not sell your data).

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information as long as your account is active.

Account Deletion: You may delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the App Settings or by contacting us at SmoothChillPill@gmail.com. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.
